题目原文：The diagram presents the process of recycling glass bottles.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

The flow chart illustrates the main steps involved in the process of recycling glass bottles.

15 words

Overall there are three main stages in the process of making glass bottles and recycling. Beginning with the collection of the bottles, then they are cleaned and reshaped, finally, these bottles are recycled and sold in supermarkets.

37 words

In the first stage of the whole process, the glass bottles are gathered at a collection point before being transported to the cleaning plant. Following this, these bottles are cleaned with high-pressure water and are separated into different colors: clear, green and brown.

43 words

Subsequently, after the bottles of three colors are processed in the recycling plant, they undergo the furnace where they are transformed into liquid glass. Next, both recycled and new liquid glass are poured in moulds to form their final shape. Once they are moulded, they ultimately become commercial items again and are sold to customers in supermarkets.

开头段（Introduction / Paraphrase）

The flow chart illustrates the main steps → stages 词汇提升 stages 比 steps 更适合带阶段划分的流程图，且与下一段 "three main stages" 形成首尾呼应 involved in the process of recycling glass bottles → recycling used glass bottles 词汇提升 原句 "the process of recycling glass bottles" 与首句 "flow chart" 在语义上有冗余——flow chart 本身就是 process；改为动名词短语更简洁；加 used 明确瓶子的回收属性.
句子提升
改写为："The flow chart illustrates the main stages involved in recycling used glass bottles."
原因：保留用户原有结构，仅做最小改动——压缩冗余、强化用词精准度，并通过 stages 与下一段关键词形成 paraphrase 之外的内部呼应。

概述段（Overview）⚠️ 本段需较大幅度重写

⚠️ 此段存在两类核心问题：① "Beginning with..." 是分词短语不能独立成句（sentence fragment），后续用三个逗号连接三个分句构成 comma splice；② 未点明流程的循环性（图中明显呈现 customer supermarket → 回收 → 再回到 customer supermarket 的循环结构）。必须整段重写。

原文中的具体错误：
"Overall there are three main stages..." → "Overall, there are..."（缺少 Overall 后的逗号）
Overall 作为副词置于句首必须跟逗号
"the process of making glass bottles and recycling" → "the recycling process"
making 和 recycling 不构成平行逻辑——本流程不是"制造 + 回收"，而是"回收"这一个完整过程
"Beginning with the collection..., then they are..., finally, these bottles..." → 需整段重写为完整复合句
分词短语不能独立成句（sentence fragment）；多个分句仅用逗号连接构成 comma splice
未点明循环性 + "reshaped" 不准确 → 必须显性表达循环（"completing a full cycle"）；瓶子在 Stage 2 是被清洗和分类，重新塑形发生在 Stage 3
流程图（含循环结构）的 Overview 必须显性表达循环性，否则 TA 受限
句子提升（整段重写）
"Overall, the recycling process involves three main stages: the collection of used bottles from supermarkets, their cleaning and sorting at the cleaning plant, and their reprocessing at the recycling plant before the finished products are returned to consumers, thus completing a full cycle."
原因：(1) 使用流程图固定 Overview 结构 "Overall, ... three main stages: ..."（与 Life Cycle of Salmon 模板一致，沿用已固化的流程图 Overview 模板）；(2) 用冒号 + 三个并列名词短语整齐覆盖三阶段；(3) 末尾 "thus completing a full cycle" 显性点出循环性，呼应图中 customer supermarket 的回流箭头——直接触发 TA 高分要素；(4) 消除所有语法错误。

主体段一（Body Paragraph 1）— Stage 1 & 2：收集与清洗分类

In the first stage of the whole process 词汇提升 "of the whole process" 与 "first stage" 语义重叠，删除后更紧凑, the glass bottles → used glass bottles 词汇提升 明确瓶子是用过的，呼应 Overview "used bottles"，避免歧义 are gathered at a collection point, → , typically from customer supermarkets, 内容补充 图中 STAGE 1 明确标注 CUSTOMER SUPERMARKET 是源头，添加这一信息使数据覆盖更完整 before being transported to → by truck to 词汇提升 图中明确画有卡车 icon，加入 "by truck" 使数据描述更精准 the cleaning plant. Following this, these → the 词汇提升 避免与上一句 "the glass bottles" 重复使用 these；定冠词 the 在已建立指称的语境中更地道 bottles are cleaned → washed 词汇提升 washed 在描述高压水冲洗时比 cleaned 更精准、更视觉化 with high-pressure water and are separated into different colors: clear, green and brown. → sorted by colour into three categories: clear, green and brown. 语法纠正 + 词汇提升 ① 删除冗余的第二个 are，让 washed 和 sorted 共享同一个 are 构成更整齐的并列结构；② "separated into different colors" 字面意思是"被分成不同颜色"逻辑别扭，"sorted by colour into three categories" 更精准——按颜色分拣到三类中
句子提升（整段优化）
"In the first stage, used glass bottles are gathered at a collection point, typically from customer supermarkets, before being transported by truck to the cleaning plant. Following this, the bottles are washed with high-pressure water and sorted by colour into three categories: clear, green and brown."
原因：保留用户原有结构和衔接序列（In the first stage / Following this），重点在三方面优化——(1) 补充图中遗漏的视觉细节（customer supermarkets 来源、truck 运输方式）；(2) 精准化用词（cleaned → washed、separated into different colors → sorted by colour into three categories）；(3) 消除冗余 are，使并列结构更整齐。

主体段二（Body Paragraph 2）— Stage 3：熔化、塑形与回流

⚠️ 本段存在多处用词搭配错误和介词错误，需要较大幅度修改。

原文中的具体错误：
"Subsequently, after the bottles..." → "In the final stage, the sorted bottles..."
Subsequently 和 after 都表示时间先后，连用属于双重表达；"bottles of three colors" 字面意思别扭，应为 "the sorted bottles"
"they undergo the furnace" → "they are melted in a furnace"
undergo 后接抽象名词如 undergo a process / a change，不能接物理对象 furnace；正确表达为 "are sent to the furnace" 或 "are melted in a furnace"
"poured in moulds" → "poured into moulds"
pour 表示"倒入"必须搭配 into 表示运动方向；in 仅表示静止位置
"to form their final shape" → "to be shaped into new bottles"
"to form their final shape" 表达模糊，未明确最终成品；"to be shaped into new bottles" 直接点明产物
"they ultimately become commercial items again" → "the freshly made bottles are returned to customer supermarkets for resale"
"commercial items" 是商业类商品的泛指，过于宽泛；应明确为 "new bottles" 或 "freshly made bottles"
未点明循环性 → 补充 "and the cycle begins anew"
本段是流程结尾，应明确呼应 Overview 中的 "completing a full cycle"，使首尾呼应——这是流程图（循环结构）拿高分的标志性写法
句子提升（整段重写）
"In the final stage, the sorted bottles are sent to the recycling plant, where they are melted in a furnace and transformed into liquid glass. This recycled liquid is then combined with new liquid glass and poured into moulds to be shaped into new bottles. Finally, the freshly made bottles are returned to customer supermarkets for resale, and the cycle begins anew."
原因：(1) 用 "In the final stage" 替代冗余的 "Subsequently, after..."，与上一段 "In the first stage" 形成整齐的阶段序列；(2) "are sent to the recycling plant, where they are melted in a furnace" 用 where 从句串起两个动作，比原文两个独立分句更流畅；(3) 修复所有介词和搭配错误；(4) 结尾 "the cycle begins anew" 显性点明循环性，与 Overview "completing a full cycle" 首尾呼应。

内容与结构建议

建议 1：循环性必须显性表达
本图最关键的特征之一是 "customer supermarket → 收集 → 处理 → 重新回到 customer supermarket" 的循环结构。Overview 和文末必须显性点明（如 "completing a full cycle" / "the cycle begins anew"），否则 TA 受限于 6 分。这一规则同样适用于 Life Cycle of Salmon 等所有循环结构流程图。
建议 2：Overview 句式必须完整
分词短语（如 "Beginning with..."）不能独立成句，必须并入主句作状语；多个分句之间不能仅用逗号连接，必须使用句号、分号或并列连词。本次的 "Beginning with the collection..., then they are cleaned..., finally, these bottles..." 同时存在 sentence fragment 和 comma splice 两类错误，应高度重视。
建议 3：图中所有视觉信息都应被利用
图中明确画出了 truck（运输工具）和 CUSTOMER SUPERMARKET（来源 + 终点），这些视觉信息可以转化为文字描述，丰富数据覆盖（如 "by truck"、"from customer supermarkets"、"returned to customer supermarkets"）。流程图写作中"看图说话"的精细程度直接影响 TA 分数。
建议 4：动词搭配的精准性
流程图常见的"被动加工"动作有固定搭配——be transported to / be washed with / be melted in / be poured into / be shaped into / be sent to。原文 "undergo the furnace" 和 "poured in moulds" 都属于固定搭配错误，建议建立"流程图动词搭配清单"专门记忆。
建议 5：段落"阶段化"标识
使用 "In the first stage / In the final stage" 这种显性的阶段标识比 Subsequently / Next 更适合流程图——一来与 Overview 的 "three main stages" 呼应，二来读者能立刻定位每段对应的图中位置。
