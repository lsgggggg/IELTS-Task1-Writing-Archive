Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

The diagram illustrates the life cycle of salmon.

开头段（Introduction / Paraphrase）

The diagram illustrates the life cycle of salmon. → The diagram illustrates the various stages in the life cycle of the salmon, a species of large fish. 句子提升 原句过于简短且与题目雷同；补充 "the various stages" 呼应流程图本质，并用同位语 "a species of large fish" 重新编码题目信息，避免 paraphrase 不充分
句子提升
改写为："The diagram illustrates the various stages in the life cycle of the salmon, a species of large fish."
原因：paraphrase 更到位，使用同位语扩展句式，避免与题目雷同。

概述段（Overview）⚠️ 本段整体重写

⚠️ Overview 只概括了"三阶段"这一层信息，并存在多处语法错误（"a eggs" 冠词 + 复数错误、"ending to" 介词错误、"Beginning..." 残句），必须整段重写。

原文中的具体错误：
"a eggs" → "eggs"（复数名词不用 a）
a/an 只修饰单数可数名词；复数直接用零冠词
"ending to a fully mature adults" → "ending with fully mature adults"（end 搭配 with，非 to）
end with 是固定搭配，end to 不成立；且 a + 复数名词冲突
"Beginning with..." 作为独立句 → "It begins with..." 或并入前句作分词状语
分词短语不能单独构成完整句，否则为句子残缺（sentence fragment）
Overview 内容单薄，只提"三阶段" → 必须同时覆盖"三阶段 + 三环境 + 循环性"
流程图（生命周期）的 Overview 三大核心：① 阶段名称 ② 环境切换 ③ 循环性
句子提升（整段重写）
"Overall, it is clear that salmon pass through three main developmental stages — fry, smolt and adult — and inhabit three distinct environments before returning to their birthplace to spawn, thus completing a full cycle."
原因：使用 "Overall, it is clear that..." 固定 Overview 引导句；用破折号嵌入三阶段术语；用 "inhabit three distinct environments" 补充"三种环境"维度；用 "thus completing a full cycle" 点明循环性——直接触发 TA 高分要素。

主体段一（Body Paragraph 1）— 从鱼卵到 fry，再迁入 lower river

Initially, salmon begin their lives as eggs, → The process begins 句子提升 用流程图固定开头句式 "The process begins with/in..." 取代 Initially，衔接更专业 which are hide under the small stones and cover by reeds → in the slow-moving waters of the upper river, where salmon eggs are laid among reeds and hidden beneath small stones 语法纠正 + 句子提升 "are hide" 被动语态错误应为 "are hidden"；"cover by" 缺 be 动词应为 "are covered by"；整合为一句并前置环境信息 where the water stream is gentle and slow, called upper river. → [并入上句：the slow-moving waters of the upper river] 句子提升 原句 "called upper river" 是残缺修饰语，信息重组更精炼 It takes approximately half a year. → for approximately five to six months 词汇提升 改为介词短语并入前句；使用图表原始数据 "5-6 months" 比 "half a year" 更精准. Following this, the eggs become to fries, → Once hatched, the young fish, now known as 'fry' 语法纠正 + 词汇提升 "become to" 动词搭配错误，become 不跟 to；"fries" 拼写错误，fry 单复数同形；"Once hatched" 分词结构更地道 which three to eight centimeters in length. → and measuring between three and eight centimetres in length, 语法纠正 原句缺系动词 are（句子残缺）；改用现在分词 measuring 避免从句冗余，更简洁 They are then move to lower river, → migrate downstream to the lower river. 语法纠正 + 词汇提升 "are then move" 主动被动混用；"migrate downstream" 比 "move to" 更精准地道；lower river 前加定冠词 the where the water current become more faster than upper river, → In this faster-flowing environment, 语法纠正 + 句子提升 "more faster" 双重比较级错误；改为 faster-flowing 呼应题目 "fast flowing" 标签 they will stay there for almost 4 years. → they remain for around four years. 词汇提升 "remain" 比 "stay" 更正式；"around four years" 更书面化；数字用英文而非阿拉伯数字
句子提升（整段重写）
"The process begins in the slow-moving waters of the upper river, where salmon eggs are laid among reeds and hidden beneath small stones for approximately five to six months. Once hatched, the young fish, now known as 'fry' and measuring between three and eight centimetres in length, migrate downstream to the lower river. In this faster-flowing environment, they remain for around four years."
原因：使用流程图固定模板；消除所有语法错误；用分词结构压缩句子，整段从 4 个零散短句变为 3 个信息密度高的复合句。

主体段二（Body Paragraph 2）— smolt 游向 open sea，长成 adult，循环开始

Once they are reaching 12 to 15 centimeters in length, which named smolts, → Upon reaching a length of 12 to 15 centimetres, the fish, at this point referred to as 'smolts', 语法纠正 + 句子提升 "are reaching" 进行时不当；"which named" 被动语态缺 are；Upon + -ing 结构比 Once they are reaching 更正式 they will swim to the open sea for nearly 5 years. → swim out to the open sea, where they spend nearly five further years maturing. 句子提升 + 词汇提升 "swim out to" 更贴近游向开放水域语义；"spend...maturing" 加入发育语义信息更丰富；用 where 从句衔接 After that, the smolts become fully mature adult salmon, with a body length of 70 to 76 centimeters, which are ready for reproduction. → By the end of this period, they have grown into fully mature adult salmon, with body lengths of 70 to 76 centimetres. 语法纠正 + 句子提升 "After that" → "By the end of this period" 更精确；become → have grown into 完成时呼应此时已长大；删除 "which are ready for reproduction" 避免与下一句 lay eggs 冗余 They return to the upper river areas to lay eggs. → These adults then return to the upper river to lay their eggs, 词汇提升 "These adults" 明确指代避免代词指代不清；删除多余 "areas"；"their eggs" 加物主代词更地道 Finaly, the life of a salmon begins anew. → and the life cycle begins anew. 语法纠正 + 词汇提升 Finaly → Finally 拼写错误，此处改用 and 连接使循环闭环更自然；"life of a salmon" → "life cycle" 术语更准确，呼应题目 life cycle
句子提升（整段重写）
"Upon reaching a length of 12 to 15 centimetres, the fish, at this point referred to as 'smolts', swim out to the open sea, where they spend nearly five further years maturing. By the end of this period, they have grown into fully mature adult salmon, with body lengths of 70 to 76 centimetres. These adults then return to the upper river to lay their eggs, and the life cycle begins anew."
原因：使用流程图循环阶段标准模板；消除所有时态、拼写、搭配错误；结尾 "the life cycle begins anew" 呼应 Overview 中的 "completing a full cycle"，形成首尾呼应。

内容与结构建议

建议 1：Overview 覆盖流程图三大维度
生命周期流程图 Overview 应概括 ① 阶段数量与名称 ② 环境变化 ③ 循环特征。原文仅概括阶段数量，TA 受限。
建议 2：使用流程图标准衔接词
The process begins with... / Once...hatched / Upon reaching... / By the end of this period... / These adults then... 比 Initially / Following this / After that / Finally 更专业。
建议 3：被动语态的规范使用
流程图中环境动作必须用完整被动语态（be + 过去分词）。原文反复出现 "are hide" / "cover by" / "are then move" 等基础错误，需系统强化。
建议 4：动物生命周期术语
fry / smolt / adult salmon 单复数同形，不可写 fries。hatch（孵化）、spawn（产卵）、mature（成熟）、migrate（迁徙）是本类题目核心词汇。
建议 5：循环性必须显性表达
若流程图为循环结构，结尾应明确用 "the cycle begins anew" / "thus completing a full cycle" 等表述，体现对循环结构的识别。
