The line chart illustrates the changes in number of shop closures and openings from 2011 to 2020 in one country.

开头段（Introduction / Paraphrase）

The line chart → graph 词汇提升 题目用的是 graph，且该图为折线图，line graph 是最准确的搭配 illustrates the changes in number → number 词汇提升 line graph 本身就展示变化，"changes in" 属于冗余表达 of shop closures and openings → new shop openings 词汇提升 补充 "new shop" 使 openings 的含义更明确 from 2011 to 2020 in one country → in one country between 2011 and 2018 语法纠正 时间范围错误（图表为 2011-2018 非 2020）；between...and 更正式；调整语序.
句子提升
改写为："The line graph illustrates the number of shop closures and new shop openings in one country between 2011 and 2018."
原因：修正时间错误，删除冗余 "changes in"，补充 "new" 使语义更明确，调整语序更流畅。

概述段（Overview）

Overall, it is clear that closed shop are more than opened shops in most of the time → shop openings declined steadily throughout the period, while closures fluctuated significantly 语法纠正 + 内容提升 原文多个语法错误（"closed shop" 缺复数、"are" 应过去时、"in most of the time" 介词错误）；且概括不准确，修改后更精确描述两条线各自的核心趋势. Additionally, there was a significant fluctuations in both choices throughout the seven year. → Notably, the two figures intersected in around 2014, after which closures generally exceeded openings. 语法纠正 + 内容提升 原句 "a significant fluctuations" 冠词与数不一致、"both choices" 用词不当、"seven year" 应为 "eight-year period"；交叉点是本图最关键的特征，替换后信息价值更高

主体段一（Body Paragraph 1）— 2011-2014 年两线收敛

⚠️ 此段存在严重问题（数据错误 + 逻辑混乱 + 多处语法/拼写错误），需要较大幅度重写。

原文中的具体错误：
"between 2011 to 2018" → "between 2011 and 2018" 或 "from 2011 to 2018"
between 必须搭配 and，不搭配 to
"with a total of 63000" → "at around 6,300"
数据严重错误，图表显示约 6,300 而非 63,000
"it overtooked closed shops" → 主语指代不清 + 拼写错误（overtook）
前文主语是 closed shops，"it overtooked closed shops" 变成自己超越自己
"remianing" → "remaining"
拼写错误
"until in 2014" + 缺少句号 → "until 2014." 多余介词 + 缺句号
until 后直接接时间，不需要 in
句子提升（整段重写）
"In 2011, the number of new shop openings stood at approximately 8,500, which was the highest figure for either category. This figure then fell consistently, reaching roughly 3,000 by 2018. Closures began lower, at around 6,300 in 2011, and also decreased gradually to meet openings at approximately 4,400 in 2014."
原因：原段逻辑混乱、数据错误严重、指代不清。重写后按时间顺序清晰描述两条线从 2011 到 2014 的走势，数据准确，逻辑清晰。

内容补充建议
用户遗漏了 2017-2018 年的数据变化——closures 在 2017 年下降到约 3,000，随后又反弹至约 5,500（2018 年）。建议补充这部分描述以使数据覆盖更完整。
句子提升（整段重写）
"Following the intersection, closures experienced dramatic volatility. In 2015, the figure plummeted to its lowest point of approximately 600, before rebounding sharply to around 5,100 in 2016. After declining again to roughly 3,000 in 2017, closures rose to approximately 5,500 in 2018. Meanwhile, openings continued their gradual descent, remaining below closures for most of this later period."
原因：原段数据有误且遗漏 2017-2018 年变化。重写后完整覆盖 2014-2018 年数据，使用了更高级的词汇（plummeted, volatility, rebounding），并在段末补充 openings 走势作为对比。

内容与结构建议

建议 1：时间范围必须准确
原文写 "from 2011 to 2020"，但图表明确显示 2011-2018。考试中必须准确读取图表的时间范围。
建议 2：数据准确性
多个数据点与图表不符（63000 应为 ~6,300；52000 应为 ~5,100；intersection 处约 4,400 而非 6,000）。建议练习精确读取图表数据。
建议 3：Overview 应概括核心趋势
Overview 应概括最显著的整体趋势，而非简单比较大小。本图最关键的特征是：(1) openings 持续下降 (2) closures 剧烈波动 (3) 两线在 2014 年交叉。
建议 4：避免代词指代不清
Body 1 中 "closed shops started with a total of 63000 and in 2012, it overtooked closed shops" 指代混乱。建议每句话明确主语。
建议 5：完整覆盖数据
2017-2018 年的数据变化被遗漏。Task 1 需要覆盖图表中所有重要的数据特征。
