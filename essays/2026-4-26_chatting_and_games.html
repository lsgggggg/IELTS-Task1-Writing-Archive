原 Overview 存在逻辑重复（两点都讲 boys vs girls 玩游戏）、词义混淆（familiar）、单复数错误（both gender）等问题，整段改写为：
"Overall, it is clear that boys devote considerably more time to games consoles than girls, with the gender gap being especially pronounced in the longest time category. In contrast, the patterns for chatting online are fairly similar between the two genders, with the majority of teenagers spending less than an hour on this activity."
原因：(1) 第一点抓 games 的最显著特征——男女差异极大（且差异在 4hrs+ 段最显著）；(2) 第二点抓 chatting 的最显著特征——男女差异小 + 集中在 <1h 段，与第一点形成对比；(3) 用 devote considerably more time / be especially pronounced / fairly similar / the majority of 等高分搭配；(4) 用 In contrast 明确两点的对比关系；(5) 完全不含具体数字。

主体段一（Body Paragraph 1）— Chatting on the internet

⚠️ 本段是失分重灾区——用户原段将 Body 1 错误聚焦到"playing 总体 vs chatting 总体"的虚构对比上，并杜撰了 68% / 62% 等图表中不存在的数据。修改方向是重置 Body 1 的功能定位——按"两组式"标准结构让 Body 1 专注 chatting 这一活动。

原文中的具体错误：
"The figures for young people who playing computer games are considerably higher than chatting online, accounting for 68 percent of youngest compared to 62 percent" → 整段删除并重写
🔴 ① **数据完全虚构** —— 图表中**根本不存在 68% / 62% 这两个数据**，本图为时长占比柱状图，每组三个时段加起来约 100%，不存在"参与 vs 不参与"的总比例。这是 Task 1 中最严重的"虚构数据"错误，会直接将 TA 限制在 5 分以下；② **young people who playing** 关系从句谓语形式错误——应为 who play / who are playing
"the duration gap of playing times is not significant, which the duration in less than an hour and 1 to 3 hours holds the highest proportion around 30% in both catagory, while 4 hour plus players only shared roughly 5%" → 整段删除并重写
① **catagory** 拼写错误——应为 categories；② **which the duration...** 关系从句结构错乱——which 后不能直接接主语；③ **holds** 主谓一致错误——主语是复数 < 1h and 1-3h；④ "around 30% in both" 数据不准——chatting <1h 实际约 53%（All），1-3h 约 35%；⑤ **shared** 用词错误（同 4-25 文章 "shared 51%" 同类问题）——share 意为"分享/分担"，无"占据"含义，应用 accounted for；⑥ "4 hour plus" 实际占比约 15%（All chatting），不是 5%
句子提升（整段重写）
本段整段删除原内容并重写为：
"In terms of chatting online, just over half of all teenagers spend less than an hour on this activity each day, while around 35% chat for one to three hours and only about 15% chat for four hours or more. The figures for boys and girls are broadly comparable, although girls are slightly more likely than boys to spend longer periods online: roughly 18% of girls chat for over four hours, compared with around 10% of boys."
原因：(1) **重置 Body 1 功能**——专注 chatting 这一活动，与 Body 2 的 gaming 形成清晰的"两组式"结构，符合 Claire 范文 "Firstly... Secondly..." / "On the one hand... On the other hand..." 模板；(2) 数据准确——All 三段时长占比（53% / 35% / 15%）与图表精确吻合；(3) 用 "In terms of..." 开头是 Task 1 主体段的固化引导句式；(4) 用 "broadly comparable" 呼应 Overview 的 "fairly similar"，建立段落呼应；(5) 末句用 "roughly 18% of girls... compared with around 10% of boys" 提供性别对比的具体数据支撑——同时修正用户末段 "55% of boys and 68% of girls" 数据 + 方向均错的问题。

主体段二（Body Paragraph 2）— Playing on games consoles

✓ 用户本段方向正确（聚焦男孩 vs 女孩玩游戏的差异），是全文表现最好的段落。但仍存在数据精度问题（32%/30%/7% vs 实际 30%/45%/25%）和"95% / 50%"虚构数据问题，需要修正。

Moreover, it is obvious that boys who play video games are nearly double those of girls → By contrast, gaming habits show a striking gender divide 词汇提升 + 句子提升 ① **Moreover** 在两组式结构中应改用 By contrast 明确对比关系（gaming 性别差异 vs chatting 性别相似）；② **it is obvious that** 主观评价句式，Task 1 应避免；③ **are nearly double those of** 中 those 指代不明；④ "show a striking gender divide" 用拟人化主语 + striking 程度词，是描述显著差异的高分句式.

About 95 percent of boys recorded in the chat play on game consoles, whereas just over 50 percent of girls have this habit → [整句删除——数据完全虚构] 内容修正 🔴 **数据完全虚构** —— 图表中**没有"是否玩"的总参与率数据**，所有柱状只表示时长段占比；"95%" / "50%" 是用户基于错误图表理解杜撰的；"have this habit" 也是不当评价语。整句删除.

More specifically, the percentages of boys who spend more than 4 hours, 1 to 3 hours and less than 1 hour on these games are 32%, 30%, and 7%, respectively → Approximately 30% of boys play on games consoles for more than four hours and a further 45% play for one to three hours, meaning that around three-quarters of boys spend at least an hour gaming each day 数据修正 + 句子提升 ① **数据精度错误** —— Boys 实际是 4hrs+ 30% / 1-3h 45% / <1h 25%（不是 32%/30%/7%）；② "More specifically" 在新段中作转折稍弱，改为直接陈述 + 用 "Approximately" 软化；③ 末尾添加 "meaning that around three-quarters of boys spend at least an hour gaming each day" 用归纳句把 30% + 45% = 75% 的合计含义点出，是 Task 1 "select main features" 而非机械罗列的高分体现.

Whereas, the gender gap narrowed in chatting online, with 55% of boys and 68% of girls → Girls show the opposite pattern, with over half playing for less than an hour and only about 5% playing for four hours or more 结构调整 + 数据修正 + 内容修正 🔴 ① **结构错位 + 数据错误** —— 原句末段拐回 chatting 段落（不该在 Body 2 出现），且 "55% of boys / 68% of girls" 数据 + 比较方向均错；② **Whereas 不能放在句首作连接词独立成句**——whereas 是从属连词，不能像 However 一样独立用；③ 修改后让 Body 2 完整聚焦 gaming，写女孩的 gaming 数据形成对比——"Girls show the opposite pattern" 与上句的 boys 描述对仗；④ 数据精确：Girls gaming <1h 约 55% / 4hrs+ 约 5%.
句子提升（整段重写）
本段整段重写为：
"By contrast, gaming habits show a striking gender divide. Approximately 30% of boys play on games consoles for more than four hours and a further 45% play for one to three hours, meaning that around three-quarters of boys spend at least an hour gaming each day. Girls show the opposite pattern, with over half playing for less than an hour and only about 5% playing for four hours or more."
原因：(1) 段落开头 "By contrast" 与 Body 1 的 "In terms of chatting online" 形成清晰的两组对比结构；(2) "show a striking gender divide" 是数据对比段的高分主题句，一句话定调本段；(3) 数据精确——Boys (4hrs+ 30% / 1-3h 45%) + Girls (<1h 55% / 4hrs+ 5%) 全部与图表吻合；(4) 用 "meaning that around three-quarters of boys..." 这一**归纳句式**展示数据合计的意义；(5) 用 "show the opposite pattern" 建立 Boys / Girls 内部对比，与首句的 "gender divide" 呼应；(6) 完全删除原段中"95% / 50% / 55% boys 68% girls" 等三处虚构 + 错位数据。

内容与结构建议

建议 1：数据准确性 — Task 1 的"高压线" 🔴
原文出现至少六处虚构 / 错误数据：① "68% play computer games" / ② "62% chat online"（根本不存在的总参与率）；③ "around 30% in both category" 4hrs+ "5%"（chatting 各时长段实际 53%/35%/15%）；④ "32%, 30%, 7% for boys gaming"（实际 30%, 45%, 25%）；⑤ "95% of boys play / 50% of girls play"（同样不存在的总参与率）；⑥ "55% of boys and 68% of girls" chatting（数据 + 性别均错位）。

核心错误根源：用户对柱状图的本质识别错误——本图描述的是"在某活动上花 X 时长的人占该群体的百分比"，**不是**"是否参与某活动的总比例"。每组（如 Boys chatting）的三个时长段加起来约 100%，覆盖该群体所有人。

固化方法：建立"读图三步法"——① 先看 Y 轴单位（本图 Percentages 0-90）；② 再看图例（4hrs+ / 1-3h / <1h 是时长段，不是参与率）；③ 最后心算每组三段加总应接近 100%。这套方法可永久复用于所有"分组 + 多类别"的柱状图。
建议 2：两组式结构（two-by-two）的标准模板
本题是典型的"两种活动 × 三组对象 × 三个时段"结构，最清晰的写法是两组式——Body 1 专写一种活动（chatting），Body 2 专写另一种活动（gaming），每段内再展开"All / Boys / Girls"三层对比。

用户原文结构错位：Body 1（段3）写"playing 总体" + "两类别共同的 30% / 5%"——既不是 chatting 段也不是 gaming 段；Body 2（段4）开头写 gaming，但末尾又拐回 chatting。修改后严格按"Body 1 = chatting / Body 2 = gaming"，结构清晰，数据各归其位。

固化模板：
- Body 1: "In terms of [活动一], [All 三段时长占比]. The figures for boys and girls are [comparable / different], although [一句性别对比]."
- Body 2: "By contrast, [活动二] [show a striking gender divide / show a similar pattern]. [Boys 数据]. Girls show [the opposite / a similar] pattern, with [Girls 数据]."
建议 3：百分比对比的固化句式
原文使用了 "shared 51% / holds 45% percentage / 32%, 30%, and 7%, respectively" 等多种不规范表达。统一为以下三类高分句式：
- 单一占比：approximately 30% of boys play... ✓
- 两数对比：roughly 18% of girls chat for over four hours, compared with around 10% of boys ✓
- 合计归纳：around three-quarters of boys spend at least an hour gaming（30% + 45% = 75% 合计）✓

三类句式可永久复用于柱状图、饼图、表格等所有涉及百分比的题型。
建议 4：术语精确——games consoles vs video games
原文用 "video games" 替代题目原词 "games consoles" —— video games 涵盖手机/电脑/掌机所有平台，而 games consoles 特指家用游戏机（PS / Xbox / Switch 等）。Task 1 改述时专业术语必须保留，只改动通用词汇即可。其他例子：
- 题目说 chatting on the Internet → 改述时保留 chatting on the internet（不要改成 instant messaging）
- 题目说 average school day → 保留 average school day（不要改成 typical day）
建议 5：Overview 的"两层对比"原则
本题图表的最显著特征是 gaming 性别差异极大 + chatting 性别差异极小 —— 这是一组天然的对比关系，应在 Overview 中同时点出，形成"双层对比"。

用户 Overview 第一点方向跑偏（"play games vs chatting" 总比例对比），第二点又重复了"男孩 games 多于女孩" —— 两点没有形成对比。修改后第一点抓 gaming（差异大），第二点抓 chatting（差异小且集中在 <1h），用 In contrast 衔接，是对比类柱状图 Overview 的固化模板。
