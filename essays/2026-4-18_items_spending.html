题目原文：The table below gives information on consumer spending on different items in five different countries in 2002.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

The table illustrates various items and statistic data on consumer spending, including five different countries and seven types of categories in 2002.

22 words

Overall, it is clear that food, drinks and tobacco held the largest portion among all categories in every countries, while the smallest share went to leisure and education. Notably, Turkey citizens spent considerably more on all three categories than those in other nations.

43 words

When it comes to Food, drinks and tobacco, Turkey recorded the highest counts among the five nations at 32.14%, followed by Ireland at nearly 29%. By contrast, Sweden had the lowest figures, with a total 15.77%, while Italy and Spain had similar spending at 16.36% and 18.80% of its total expenditure. As for clothing and footwear, most countries were at a broadly close level, at around 6%, while Italy ranked first at 9%.

73 words

Regarding leisure and education, which was the least popular types with the minimal percentage of spending in all five countries. Turkey still led with 4.35%, conversely, Spain had the smallest at just 1.98%. Moreover, the remaining countries, namely Ireland, Italy and Sweden, spent a similar percentage, at roughly 3%.

开头段（Introduction / Paraphrase）

The table illustrates various items and statistic data on consumer spending, including five different countries and seven types of categories in 2002 → the percentage of consumer spending on three categories of items across five countries in 2002 语法纠正 + 词汇提升 + 事实纠正 ① "seven types" 是严重事实错误——表格只有 3 类；② "various items and statistic data" 冗余，"statistic data" 应为 "statistical data" 或直接改用 percentage；③ "including five different countries" 语法结构不通——including 后应跟被 illustrate 的对象；④ 改用 across 替代 including，语义更紧凑.
句子提升（整段改写）
"The line graph illustrates the percentage of consumer spending on three categories of items across five countries in 2002."
原因：修正 "seven types" 这个重大事实错误，删除冗余表达，使用 percentage 直接点明数据单位，句式紧凑地道，与范文 "The table illustrates..." 模板保持一致。

概述段（Overview）⚠️ 最关键

Overall, it is clear that food, drinks and tobacco held the largest portion → accounted for the largest proportion 词汇提升 account for 是占比描述的标准搭配，比 held the largest portion 更地道；proportion 比 portion 在学术写作中更常见 among all categories in every countries → every country 语法纠正 every 后必须接单数名词, while the smallest share went to leisure and education. Notably, Turkey citizens spent considerably more on all three categories than those in other nations → Turkey led in two of the three categories 语法纠正 + 事实纠正 + 词汇提升 ① "Turkey citizens" 应为 "Turkish citizens"；② 严重事实错误——Turkey 只在 Food/Drinks/Tobacco 和 Leisure/Education 两类领先，Clothing/Footwear 中 Italy 9% 才是最高，必须改为 "two of the three"；③ "than those in other nations" 代词 those 指代不清.
句子提升（整段改写）
"Overall, it is clear that food, drinks and tobacco accounted for the largest proportion of spending in every country, while leisure and education received the smallest share. Notably, Turkey led in two of the three categories."
原因：保留用户原句 "Overall, it is clear that" 来自范文模板的开头（用户已采纳的好模板不动），使用 accounted for / received the smallest share 两个占比类高级搭配，用 "led in two of the three" 准确概括 Turkey 的地位。

主体段一（Body Paragraph 1）— Food/Drinks/Tobacco 和 Clothing/Footwear

When it comes to Food, drinks and tobacco → Regarding food, drinks and tobacco 词汇提升 Regarding 比 When it comes to 更正式简练；与主体段二的 As for 形成稳定的衔接词模板, Turkey recorded the highest counts → highest percentage 词汇提升 表格展示的是 percentage 而非 counts；counts 指"计数"，与百分比数据不匹配 among the five nations at 32.14%, followed by Ireland at nearly 29%. By contrast, Sweden had the lowest figures, with a total 15.77% → figure at just 15.77% 语法纠正 + 词汇提升 ① 单一数据应为单数 figure；② "with a total 15.77%" 冠词 a 与后接短语不搭，且 total 用于单个数据点冗余；③ 改用 at just 15.77% 使数据引用简洁, while Italy and Spain had similar spending at 16.36% and 18.80% of its total expenditure → fell in between, at 16.36% and 18.80% respectively 语法纠正 + 词汇提升 ① its 指代错误——应为 their 指代五国；② 16.36% 与 18.80% 差距实际不小，称 similar spending 不准确，改为 fell in between 更客观；③ 用 respectively 明确数据对应关系. As for clothing and footwear, most countries were at a broadly close level, at around 6% → stood at a broadly close level of around 6% 词汇提升 stood at 是数据引用的高分搭配，比 were at 更正式；用 of 替代第二个 at 避免介词堆叠, while → whereas 词汇提升 whereas 在对比语境中比 while 更正式，且避免与前文 while 重复 Italy ranked first at 9%.
句子提升（整段改写）
"Regarding food, drinks and tobacco, Turkey recorded the highest percentage at 32.14%, followed by Ireland at nearly 29%. By contrast, Sweden had the lowest figure at just 15.77%, while Italy and Spain fell in between at 16.36% and 18.80% respectively. For clothing and footwear, most countries stood at a broadly close level of around 6%, whereas Italy ranked first at 9%."
原因：保留用户 "Turkey 最高 → Ireland → Sweden 最低 → Italy/Spain 中间" 的清晰逻辑，修正所有语法和事实错误，使用 stood at / fell in between / ranked first 等地道的数据描述搭配。

主体段二（Body Paragraph 2）— Leisure/Education

Regarding leisure and education, which was the least popular types with the minimal percentage of spending in all five countries → As for leisure and education, this was the least popular category in all five countries 语法纠正 + 词汇提升 ① 严重残句！原文 "Regarding X, which was..." 中 which 引导从句却无主句；② the least popular types 应为单数 category（此处只有一个类别）；③ the minimal percentage 中 minimal 使用不当，应为 smallest；④ 精简字数，将冗余描述合并. Turkey still led with 4.35%, conversely, Spain had the smallest → whereas Spain had the smallest proportion 语法纠正 + 词汇提升 ① 严重粘连句（comma splice）！两个独立句不能用逗号连接；② 改为 whereas 从句使其成为合法的主从复合句；③ 补充 proportion 使 "the smallest" 指代明确 at just 1.98%. Moreover, the remaining countries, namely Ireland, Italy and Sweden, spent a similar percentage, at roughly 3% → The remaining three countries — Ireland, Italy and Sweden — spent a similar amount of roughly 3% 词汇提升 ① Moreover 在此为并列补充，改用直接陈述更简洁；② 用破折号替代逗号嵌入同位语，句式更高阶；③ a similar percentage, at roughly 3% 中介词短语结构松散，改为 a similar amount of roughly 3%.
句子提升（整段改写）
"As for leisure and education, this was the least popular category in all five countries. Turkey still led with 4.35%, whereas Spain had the smallest proportion at just 1.98%. The remaining three countries — Ireland, Italy and Sweden — spent a similar amount of roughly 3%."
原因：修复残句和粘连句两处严重语法问题，使用 whereas / 破折号同位语等更高阶的句式，保留用户 "Turkey 最高 → Spain 最低 → 其余相近" 的清晰逻辑。

内容与结构建议

建议 1：事实核对是 Task 1 的生命线
原文开头写 "seven types of categories"、概述段说 Turkey 在 "all three categories" 领先，这两处事实错误直接拉低 TA 评分。写 Task 1 时必须先数清楚类别、对象、时间点，确认每一处"最高/最低/唯一"的表述与图表一致。
建议 2：Overview 应抓住"反常特征"
本题除了"Food 最大、Leisure 最小"这个明显特征外，还有一个反常特征——Italy 在 Clothing/Footwear 上达 9%，明显高于其他国家。建议在 Overview 或主体段中明确点出这个异常，可进一步提升 TA。
建议 3："similar" 的使用门槛
16.36% 与 18.80% 之间有 2.4 个百分点的差距，称 "similar" 不够精确；3.20%、3.22%、2.21% 之间的差距更大，勉强算 similar。使用 "similar" 前应判断差距是否真的可忽略。
建议 4：字数控制
原文 187 词超出上限 7 词，修改后精简至 159 词。考试中一旦发现超出，应优先删除重复副词、冗余修饰语，而非砍掉数据点。
建议 5：衔接词避免重复
原文开头用 "When it comes to food..."、主体段二又用 "Regarding leisure..." 衔接词显得随意。本次修改将主体段一改用 "Regarding"，主体段二改用 "As for"，让两段形成呼应而非重复，可固化为"表格题衔接词模板"。
