题目原文：The table below gives information on consumer spending on different items in five different countries in 2002.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

The table illustrates various items and statistic data on consumer spending, including five different countries and seven types of categories in 2002.

22 words

Overall, it is clear that food, drinks and tobacco held the largest portion among all categories in every countries, while the smallest share went to leisure and education. Notably, Turkey citizens spent considerably more on all three categories than those in other nations.

43 words

By contrast, Sweden had the lowest figures, with a total 15.77%, while Italy and Spain had similar spending at 16.36% and 18.80% of its total expenditure. As for clothing and footwear, most countries were at a broadly close level, at around 6%, while Italy ranked first at 9%.

73 words

Turkey still led with 4.35%, conversely, Spain had the smallest at just 1.98%. Moreover, the remaining countries, namely Ireland, Italy and Sweden, spent a similar percentage, at roughly 3%.

开头段（Introduction / Paraphrase）

The table illustrates various items and statistic data on consumer spending, including → the percentage of consumer spending on three different categories of items across 语法纠正 + 词汇提升 + 事实纠正 ① 删除冗余的 "various items and statistic data"；② "statistic data" 应为 "statistical data" 或直接改用 "percentage"；③ 事实纠正——把后文的 "seven" 错误源头提到前半句用 "three different categories of items" 替代；④ 用 "across" 替代 "including"，与国家描述衔接更自然；⑤ 保留用户的 "different" 修饰词 five different countries and seven types of categories in 2002 → five different countries in 2002 事实纠正 表格实际只有 3 个类别，"seven types of categories" 是事实错误。类别信息已移至前半句，这里保留用户原有的 "five different countries".
句子提升（整段改写）
"The table illustrates the percentage of consumer spending on three different categories of items across five different countries in 2002."
原因：修正 "seven" 事实错误，删除冗余表达，保留用户的 "different" 修饰词，句式与范文 "The table illustrates..." 模板保持一致。

概述段（Overview）⚠️ 最关键

Overall, it is clear that food, drinks and tobacco held the largest portion → accounted for the largest proportion 词汇提升 account for 是占比描述的标准搭配，比 held the largest portion 更地道；proportion 比 portion 在学术写作中更常见 among all categories in every countries → every country 语法纠正 every 后必须接单数名词, while the smallest share went to leisure and education. Notably, Turkey citizens → Turkey 语法纠正 国家名不能直接作定语修饰复数名词，应为 Turkish citizens，或直接用 Turkey 作主语更简洁 spent considerably more on all three categories than those in other nations → than the other nations, leading in two of the three categories 事实纠正 + 词汇提升 ① 严重事实错误——Turkey 只在 Food/Drinks/Tobacco 和 Leisure/Education 两类领先，Clothing/Footwear 中 Italy 9% 才是最高；② 保留用户原文核心对比 "spent considerably more than the other nations"；③ 用分词短语 leading in two of the three categories 补充具体领先范围；④ "than those in other nations" 中 those 指代不清，合并到主句.
句子提升（整段改写）
"Overall, it is clear that food, drinks and tobacco accounted for the largest proportion of spending among all categories in every country, while leisure and education received the smallest share. Notably, Turkey spent considerably more than the other nations, leading in two of the three categories."
原因：保留用户原句 "Overall, it is clear that"（来自范文模板）和核心对比 "spent considerably more than the other nations"，使用 accounted for / received the smallest share 两个占比搭配，修正 "all three" → "two of the three" 的事实错误。

主体段一（Body Paragraph 1）— Food/Drinks/Tobacco 和 Clothing/Footwear

When it comes to Food, drinks and tobacco → When it comes to food, drinks and tobacco 语法纠正 句中不需要首字母大写 "Food"；保留用户原本使用的 "When it comes to" 衔接词，不做替换, Turkey recorded the highest counts → highest percentage 词汇提升 表格展示的是 percentage 而非 counts；counts 指"计数"，与百分比数据不匹配 among the five nations at 32.14%, followed by Ireland at nearly 29%. By contrast, Sweden had the lowest figures, with a total 15.77% → figure at just 15.77% 语法纠正 + 词汇提升 ① 单一国家的单一数据应为单数 figure；② "with a total 15.77%" 冠词 a 与后接短语不搭，且 total 用于单个数据点冗余；③ 改用 at just 15.77% 使数据引用简洁, while Italy and Spain had similar spending at 16.36% and 18.80% of its total expenditure → fell in between at 16.36% and 18.80% of their total expenditure respectively 语法纠正 + 词汇提升 + 信息保留 ① its 指代错误——应为 their 指代 Italy 和 Spain 两国；② 16.36% 与 18.80% 差距实际不小，称 similar spending 不准确，改为 fell in between 更客观；③ 补充 respectively 明确数据对应关系；④ 完整保留用户原文的 "of their total expenditure" 信息点. As for clothing and footwear, most countries were at a broadly close level, at around 6% → stood at a broadly close level of around 6% 词汇提升 stood at 是数据引用的高分搭配，比 were at 更正式；用 of 替代第二个 at 避免介词堆叠, while → whereas 词汇提升 whereas 在对比语境中比 while 更正式，且避免与前文 while 重复 Italy ranked first at 9%.
句子提升（整段改写）
"When it comes to food, drinks and tobacco, Turkey recorded the highest percentage among the five nations at 32.14%, followed by Ireland at nearly 29%. By contrast, Sweden had the lowest figure at just 15.77%, while Italy and Spain fell in between at 16.36% and 18.80% of their total expenditure respectively. As for clothing and footwear, most countries stood at a broadly close level of around 6%, whereas Italy ranked first at 9%."
原因：完整保留用户的 "When it comes to... As for..." 衔接词系统、"among the five nations"、"of their total expenditure" 等信息点，修正所有语法和事实错误，使用 stood at / fell in between / ranked first 等地道搭配。

主体段二（Body Paragraph 2）— Leisure/Education

Regarding leisure and education, which was the least popular types with the minimal percentage of spending in all five countries → Regarding leisure and education, this was the least popular category, with the smallest percentage of spending in all five countries 语法纠正 + 词汇提升 + 信息保留 ① 严重残句！原文 "Regarding X, which was..." 中 which 引导从句却无主句，改为 this was 使其成为独立句；② the least popular types 应为单数 category；③ the minimal percentage 中 minimal 使用不自然，改为 smallest；④ 完整保留用户原文的 "with the smallest percentage of spending in all five countries" 信息点；⑤ 保留 "Regarding" 这个用户原本使用的衔接词. Turkey still led with 4.35%, conversely, Spain had the smallest → whereas Spain had the smallest proportion 语法纠正 + 词汇提升 ① 严重粘连句！两个独立句不能只用逗号连接，conversely 是副词不能连接独立句；② 改为 whereas 从句使其成为合法的主从复合句；③ 补充 proportion 使 "the smallest" 指代明确 at just 1.98%. Moreover, the remaining countries , namely Ireland, Italy and Sweden, → — namely Ireland, Italy and Sweden — 句式提升 用破折号替代逗号嵌入 namely 同位语，句式更高阶；保留用户原文的 "Moreover" 衔接词和 "namely" 同位语标记 spent a similar percentage, at → percentage of 语法纠正 "a similar percentage, at roughly 3%" 中介词短语结构松散，改为 "a similar percentage of roughly 3%" 使介词搭配更紧凑 roughly 3%.
句子提升（整段改写）
"Regarding leisure and education, this was the least popular category, with the smallest percentage of spending in all five countries. Turkey still led with 4.35%, whereas Spain had the smallest proportion at just 1.98%. Moreover, the remaining countries — namely Ireland, Italy and Sweden — spent a similar percentage of roughly 3%."
原因：修复残句和粘连句两处严重语法问题；完整保留用户原文的 "with the smallest percentage of spending in all five countries"、"Moreover"、"namely" 等所有信息点和衔接词；仅在句式（破折号同位语）和个别词汇层面升级。

内容与结构建议

建议 1：事实核对是 Task 1 的生命线
原文开头写 "seven types of categories"、概述段说 Turkey 在 "all three categories" 领先，这两处事实错误直接拉低 TA 评分。写 Task 1 时必须先数清楚类别、对象、时间点，确认每一处"最高/最低/唯一/全部"的表述与图表一致。
建议 2：Overview 可进一步抓住"反常特征"
本题除了"Food 最大、Leisure 最小"这个明显特征外，还有一个反常特征——Italy 在 Clothing/Footwear 上达 9%，明显高于其他国家。可以考虑在 Overview 或主体段中明确点出这个异常，进一步提升 TA。
建议 3："similar" 的使用门槛
16.36% 与 18.80% 之间有 2.4 个百分点的差距，称 "similar" 不够精确；3.20%、3.22%、2.21% 之间的差距勉强算 similar。使用 "similar" 前应判断差距是否真的可忽略。
建议 4：衔接词系统可以固化
你在本文中使用了 "When it comes to... By contrast... As for... Regarding... Moreover" 的衔接词系统，覆盖了引入、对比、转换话题、追加等场景，结构意识很好。后续表格题可以继续用这套衔接词，形成稳定的个人模板。
建议 5：残句陷阱
"Regarding X, which was..." 这种 "Regarding + 名词, which + 从句" 的结构容易写成残句，因为 which 引导的是从句，没有主句。要么改为 "Regarding X, this was..."（独立句），要么 "Regarding X, Y, which was..."（Y 作主语）。
