题目原文：The graph below gives information about the percentage of the population in 4 Asian countries living in cities from 1970 to 2020, with predictions for 2030 and 2040.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

The line chart illustrates and predicts the proportion of urban residents in four Asian countries, including the Philippines, Malaysia, Thailand, and Indonesia, between 1970 and 2040.

However, in the next 40 years, the urban population percentage in Philippines fluctuated, until in 1992 when Malaysia secondly surpassed the Philippines at about 48% and maintained its top ranking. Over time, the figures for Indonesia rose steadily, overtaking Thailand in 2000 and Philippines in 2010, becoming the second-highest, just below the Malaysia. Thailand's population grew fast at first, then reached a plateau from 1990 to 2020, while this country was expected to have a rapid increase after 2020 to 2040.

概述段（Overview）⚠️ 存在事实性错误，需较大幅度修正

⚠️ 本段存在 Task 1 最严重类型的错误——对图表主题的**事实性误读**：原句 "had the largest population" 意为"人口总量最大"，但本图展示的是 "percentage of the population living in cities"（**城市化率**），不是人口总量。这一错误会直接触发 TA 失分。

内容与结构建议

建议 1：Overview 核心事实性错误（最严重）
原 Overview 中 "the Malaysia had the largest population for most of the period" 存在两层错误——① "the Malaysia" 国家名前误加 the；② "largest population"（人口总量）与图表主题（"percentage of population living in cities"，即**城市化率**）完全不符。这种对图表主题的误读会直接触发 TA 失分。建议建立"读图前先读图表标题和 Y 轴标签"的固定习惯，确保主题词（此处为 urbanisation rate）贯穿全文。
建议 2：国家名冠词的系统性问题
**the Philippines**（必须带 the）、**Malaysia / Thailand / Indonesia**（不带 the）是本题的国家名使用规则。原文中 "the Philippines" 在 5 处被误写为 "Philippines"，而 "Malaysia" 在 2 处被误写为 "the Malaysia"，规则正好颠倒。背诵口诀：**以 -s 结尾的群岛/联邦国家（the Philippines / the Netherlands / the United States / the United Kingdom）前必须加 the；其他国家名前一般不加 the**。
建议 3：population vs. urbanisation rate 的区分
本图的四条线代表 percentage of the population living in cities，即城市化率（一个比例）。原文多次用 "population" 指代（"had the largest population" / "Thailand's population grew fast"），字面意思是"人口总量"，与图表主题不符。建议使用 "urbanisation rate" 或 "the figure for X"（专指某国该比例），避免主题词偏差。
建议 4：时态混乱（预测 vs. 历史）
本题涉及 1970-2020 历史数据（过去时）和 2030-2040 预测数据（现在时 + 被动），原文 "will be projected to reach" / "was expected to have" 均时态混乱。规则：① 预测动作本身是当下发生的，应用 "are/is projected to" 或 "are/is expected to"；② "was expected" 仅在描述过去某时刻的预期（且该预期已被证伪或已过时）时使用，本题预测至今仍有效，不能用过去时。
建议 5：数据准确性
2040 Malaysia 数值 "nearly 90%" 是明显高估——图表显示 Malaysia 2040 约 82%。Task 1 数据引用需严格对照图表刻度，宁可使用 approximately / over 80% / around 82% 等保守表达，也不要过度拔高数值。
