Overview 内容单薄 出现 1 次
▶

规则说明：Task 1 流程图（生命周期）的 Overview 必须同时覆盖三大维度：① 阶段数量与名称 ② 环境切换 ③ 循环性。只概括"几个阶段"是不够的，会被判定为部分回应。

Overall, there are three main stages in the development of a salmon. Beginning with eggs and ending with adults.（只覆盖阶段数量）
Overall, it is clear that salmon pass through three main developmental stages — fry, smolt and adult — and inhabit three distinct environments before returning to their birthplace to spawn, thus completing a full cycle.（同时覆盖三阶段 + 三环境 + 循环）
