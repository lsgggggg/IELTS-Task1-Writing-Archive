按 [静态/动态] × [图表类型] 分类的通用写作框架，包含开头改述、Overview 和数据描述模板

静态类 × 表格（单个时间点）写作模板

基于 1 篇文章总结，随着更多同类文章的积累将进一步完善

开头段模板（题目改述） ▶
The table illustrates the [数据单位，如 percentage / amount / number] of [主题，如 consumer spending / energy consumption] on [数量] categories of [对象，如 items / products] across [数量] countries in [时间点].

要点：① 用 illustrates 替换题目中的 shows / gives information on；② 用 percentage / amount / number 明确数据类型，避免题目原文的 "information on"；③ 用 across 而非 including 引导范围，更紧凑；④ 切勿盲目使用 "different" 等修饰词，除非图表有多个时间点或类型差异；⑤ 数清楚类别数量、对象数量，不可出现 "seven types" 这类事实错误

概述段模板（Overview）⚠️ 最重要 ▶
Overall, it is clear that [最大类别] accounted for the largest proportion of [主题] in every [单位，如 country / group], while [最小类别] received the smallest share. Notably, [特殊对象，如 某国 / 某组] led in [X out of Y] of the categories.

要点：① 两个维度覆盖——"全局最大/最小类别" + "某对象在多数类别中领先"；② 不出现具体数字；③ 使用 accounted for / received the smallest share 标准占比搭配；④ "led in X of the Y categories" 精确表达部分领先，避免 "in all categories" 这类易出错的泛化

主体段数据描述模板 ▶

主体段一（第一组 / 主要类别 + 次要类别对比）：

Regarding [类别A], [极值对象] recorded the highest percentage at [数据], followed by [第二名] at [数据]. By contrast, [最低对象] had the lowest figure at just [数据], while [中间对象1] and [中间对象2] fell in between at [数据] and [数据] respectively. For [类别B], most [单位] stood at a broadly close level of around [数据], whereas [异常对象] ranked first at [数据].

主体段二（第二组 / 剩余类别）：

As for [类别C], this was the least popular category in all [数量] [单位]. [领先对象] still led with [数据], whereas [最低对象] had the smallest proportion at just [数据]. The remaining [数量] [单位] — [列举] — spent a similar amount of roughly [数据].

要点：① 固定衔接词序列：Regarding → By contrast → while → For → whereas → As for → whereas → The remaining；② 数据引用固定搭配 recorded the highest percentage at / had the lowest figure at just / stood at / fell in between at / ranked first at；③ 中间数值用 respectively 明确对应；④ 用破折号嵌入同位语列举剩余对象；⑤ 当多个数据接近时，用 "stood at a broadly close level of around" 避免重复报数

动态类 × 流程图（生命周期/循环流程）写作模板

基于 1 篇文章总结，随着更多同类文章的积累将进一步完善

开头段模板（题目改述） ▶
The diagram illustrates the various stages in the [流程名称，如 life cycle] of [主语，如 the salmon], [同位语补充，如 a species of large fish].

要点：① 用 illustrates 替换 shows；② 加入 "the various stages in the..." 明确流程图本质；③ 用同位语对主语做同义补充，避免与题目雷同

概述段模板（Overview）⚠️ 最重要 ▶
Overall, it is clear that [主语] pass through [数量] main [developmental/process] stages — [阶段1], [阶段2] and [阶段3] — and [额外维度，如 inhabit three distinct environments] before [循环触发事件，如 returning to their birthplace to spawn], thus completing a full cycle.

要点：① 覆盖三大维度——阶段 + 额外特征 + 循环性；② 用破折号嵌入阶段术语；③ 用 "thus completing a full cycle" 点明循环，直接触发 TA 高分；④ 如为非循环流程，将结尾改为 "before reaching the final [output]"

主体段数据描述模板 ▶

主体段一（前半流程）：

The process begins in [起始环境], where [对象] [被动动作，如 are laid / are produced] [方位介词短语] for [时间范围]. Once [状态变化过去分词，如 hatched / formed], [新阶段对象], now known as '[术语]' and measuring [尺寸], [动作，如 migrate downstream to] [下一环境]. In this [环境特征] environment, they remain for [时长].

主体段二（后半流程 + 循环）：

Upon reaching [条件，如 a length of ...], [对象], at this point referred to as '[术语]', [动作] to [下一环境], where they spend [时长] [发育状态，如 maturing]. By the end of this period, they have grown into [最终阶段], with [尺寸数据]. These [最终阶段对象] then return to [起始环境] to [循环触发动作], and the [流程名] begins anew.

要点：① 使用固定衔接序列 The process begins → Once [过去分词] → migrate/move → Upon reaching → By the end of this period → These...then；② 环境动作必须用被动语态（are laid, are hidden）；③ 使用分词结构（measuring, hatched, referred to as）压缩句子；④ 结尾的 "begins anew" 呼应 Overview 的 "full cycle" 形成首尾呼应

动态类 × 流程图（工业循环流程）写作模板

基于 1 篇文章总结，专门用于工业生产/回收等"原料 → 加工 → 成品 → 回到起点"型循环流程

开头段模板（题目改述） ▶
The flow chart illustrates the main stages involved in [流程动作，如 recycling used glass bottles / producing X].

要点：① 用 illustrates 替换题目中的 shows / presents；② 用 "main stages involved in" 强调"阶段性"特征；③ 在动名词前加修饰词（如 used / raw）使表达更精确，避免与题目雷同

概述段模板（Overview）⚠️ 最重要 ▶
Overall, the [流程名称] process involves [数量] main stages: [阶段1：起点动作], [阶段2：中间加工], and [阶段3：成品 + 回到起点] before [最终成品] are returned to [起点对象], thus completing a full cycle.

要点：① 用冒号 + 三段式列举所有主要阶段，让 overview 一目了然；② 必须显式点明 "completing a full cycle"——这是工业循环流程拿 TA 高分的关键标志；③ 阶段描述用动名词短语保持语法一致性（the collection of... / their cleaning... / their reprocessing...）

主体段数据描述模板 ▶

主体段一（前半流程：收集 + 初步加工）：

In the first stage, [原料对象] are gathered at [起点位置], typically from [来源], before being transported by [运输方式] to [加工地点]. Following this, the [原料] are [加工动作1，如 washed with...] and [加工动作2，如 sorted by...] into [数量] categories: [类别1], [类别2] and [类别3].

主体段二（后半流程：再加工 + 循环）：

In the final stage, the sorted [原料] are sent to [再加工地点], where they are [关键加工动作，如 melted in a furnace] and transformed into [中间产物]. This [中间产物] is then combined with [新原料] and poured into [模具/容器] to be shaped into [成品]. Finally, the freshly made [成品] are returned to [起点对象] for resale, and the cycle begins anew.

要点：① 固定衔接序列 In the first stage → before being transported → Following this → In the final stage → This X is then → Finally → and the cycle begins anew；② 全段保持被动语态（are gathered / are washed / are melted / are returned）符合流程图惯例；③ 用 "transformed into" 表达"形态转化"，"combined with" 表达"原料融合"——这两个动词是工业流程的高频高分搭配；④ 结尾的 "the cycle begins anew" 与 Overview 的 "completing a full cycle" 首尾呼应，是循环流程拿高分的固定收束

动态类 × 折线图 写作模板

基于 2 篇文章总结（2 线图交叉型 + 4 线图多国对比型），模板已覆盖折线图两大子类

开头段模板（题目改述） ▶
The line graph illustrates the [数据描述，如 number of shop closures and new shop openings] in [地点描述，如 one country] between [起始年] and [结束年].

要点：① 用 illustrates 替换题目中的 shows；② between...and 替换 from...to；③ 如有多个类别，可先概括再列举（如 "in four European countries — A, B, C, and D"）

概述段模板（Overview）⚠️ 最重要 ▶
Overall, it is clear that [第一条线的核心趋势，如 shop openings declined steadily throughout the period], while [第二条线的核心趋势，如 closures fluctuated significantly]. Notably, [最显著的特征，如 the two figures intersected in around [年份], after which [后续趋势]].

要点：① 概述段不包含具体数字；② 概括 2-3 个最显著的趋势/特征；③ 如有交叉点，这是必须提及的关键特征；④ 用 Notably / In addition 引出第二个特征

主体段数据描述模板 ▶

主体段一（起始数据 + 收敛/交叉前）：

In [起始年], the number of [较高的类别] stood at approximately [数据], which was the highest figure for either category. This figure then [趋势，如 fell consistently], reaching roughly [数据] by [结束年]. [较低的类别] began lower, at around [数据] in [起始年], and also [趋势] to meet [另一类别] at approximately [数据] in [交叉年].

主体段二（交叉后至结束）：

Following the intersection, [类别] experienced [趋势描述，如 dramatic volatility]. In [年份], the figure [plummeted / surged] to [its lowest/highest point of approximately 数据], before [rebounding sharply / declining] to around [数据] in [年份]. Meanwhile, [另一类别] continued [their gradual descent/ascent], remaining [below/above] [另一类别] for most of this later period.

要点：① 用 stood at approximately / reaching roughly / at around 交替引用数据；② 用 plummeted, rebounding, volatility 等高级词汇描述剧烈变化；③ 用 Meanwhile 在段末补充另一条线的走势作为对比；④ 如有交叉点，可作为分段依据

动态类 × 折线图（多条线 + 历史 + 预测）写作模板

基于 1 篇文章总结，专门用于"4 条及以上线 + 含预测数据"型折线图，重点处理排名变化和历史/预测时态区分

开头段模板（题目改述） ▶
The line graph illustrates the [主题，如 proportion of urban residents] in [数量] [地域类别，如 Asian countries] — [逐一列出国家名称] — between [起始年] and [结束年], with projections provided for [预测年份1] and [预测年份2].

要点：① 沿用 "The line graph illustrates..." 固化开头模板；② 用破折号嵌入国家名称同位语，比 "including" 更精确；③ **必须补充 "with projections provided for X and Y"**——当题目明确指出"含预测"时，改述段必须保留"预测"这一关键信息，否则信息遗漏；④ 注意国家名冠词：the Philippines 必须带 the，Malaysia / Thailand / Indonesia 不带 the

概述段模板（Overview）⚠️ 最重要 ▶
Overall, it is clear that [全局共同趋势，如 the figures for all four nations rose considerably] over the [时长]-year period, while [例外对象的特征，如 the figure for the Philippines fluctuated the most]. Notably, [主导对象] had the [极值特征，如 highest urbanisation rate] for most of the period, establishing a clear lead from [时间锚点，如 around the 1990s onwards].

要点：① 两个 Notably 级特征——"整体共同趋势 + 主导对象的领先时间锚点"；② 用 "the figure for X" 固定搭配专指某一国家的数据，避免 figures 复数引起的歧义；③ 必须确保主题词与图表一致——如图为"城市化率"则用 urbanisation rate，不可写成 population；④ 不出现具体数字

主体段数据描述模板 ▶

主体段一（时间序列 + 排名变化）：

In [起始年], all [数量] [对象类别] recorded their lowest [主题值], and all are projected to reach their peak by [结束年]. Over the following decades, [某对象的变化], until around [关键时间点] when [对象A] surpassed [对象B] at approximately [数据] and maintained its top ranking thereafter. The figure for [对象C] rose steadily, overtaking [对象D] in around [年份] and [对象E] in around [年份] to become the second-highest. [其他对象]'s figure grew rapidly at first and then reached a plateau between [年份范围], although it is expected to rise rapidly again from [预测起点] to [预测终点].

主体段二（起止排名快照）：

In [起始年], [起点第一名] was the most [主题形容词] of the [数量] [对象类别], with just over [数据]%, followed by [第二名] ([数据]%), [第三名] ([数据]%), and [第四名] ([数据]%). Although the figures fluctuated noticeably between [前期范围], no further ranking changes are predicted from [后期起点] to [后期终点]. By the end of the period, [终点第一名] is expected to have the largest proportion at over [数据]%, followed by [第二名] at around [数据]%, [第三名] at about [数据]%, and [第四名] at just [数据]%.

要点：① **双段结构**——Body 1 走"时间轴 + 排名变化"，Body 2 走"起止快照"，两段互补而不重复；② 固定衔接序列：In [起始年] → Over the following decades → until around [关键年份] → The figure for X rose steadily, overtaking... → Although... → By the end of the period；③ **时态规则**：历史数据用过去时，预测数据用 "is/are expected to / projected to"，不能用 was expected；④ **数据引用阶梯**：起始值用 "just over [N]%"，中间值用 "at approximately [N]%"，预测值用 "at over [N]%" / "at around [N]%" / "at about [N]%" / "at just [N]%" 呈现梯度；⑤ **国家名冠词**：the Philippines 带 the，其他国家名不带 the
